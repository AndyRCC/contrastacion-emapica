# Bulk meter verification: case study

Public summary of a field campaign that verified 53 bulk water meters at a Peruvian water
utility's wells and reservoirs against a portable clamp-on ultrasonic reference. 41 meters were
tested on site, and 15 were corrected in the field and re-verified.

**View it:** https://andyrcc.github.io/contrastacion-emapica/

It covers the method and what was delivered. The client's name, station names and locations,
serial numbers, readings and individual results are intentionally left out. The chart uses
made-up numbers, and the photos were chosen so they don't show any identifying details.

Work done as Instrumentation & SCADA Engineer at LabProjects.
